# The 4-step

**Source:** Sharran Srivatsaa, CEO of Acquisition.com. He built it after asking Richard Branson, over tennis, "You run 10 companies. How do you make decisions?" The Anatomy of a Dream podcast, "The Man That Makes Billionaires," 2026-04-24, 35:39 to 36:37.

**Use when:** someone brings a problem. This is the default resolver.

**Why it works:** most people have no framework for decisions, so they make dumb ones. A fixed sequence removes the panic.

## The questions, in order

1. **Understand the context.** Where is the shared information? Ask "Tell me about that." Get the facts before opinions.
2. **Isolate the issue.** What problem are we actually solving? One sentence. If it is two problems, split them.
3. **Accept the risk.** If we make this decision, what actually breaks? Name it. Decide whether we can live with it.
4. **Map the decision.** Given all that, what is the next step? One concrete action with an owner.

- Facts:
- The one problem:
- What breaks, accepted yes / no:
- Next step + owner:

## Traps

- Skipping step 1 and solving the wrong problem.
- Answering step 3 with "nothing." Something always breaks. Find it.
