# The algorithm

**Source:** Elon Musk. Five steps, in order, as laid out in Walter Isaacson's "Elon Musk" (2023) and Musk's 2021 Starbase tour with Everyday Astronaut.

**Use when:** building, cutting, or automating a process, tool, skill, or system. Also when a tool shelf or a workflow has grown.

## The steps, in order. Never skip ahead.

1. **Make the requirement less dumb.** Every requirement has a named owner, a person, not a department. Ask who. Ask why. Requirements from smart people are the most dangerous, because nobody questions them.
2. **Delete the part or the step.** If you do not end up adding at least 10% back later, you did not delete enough.
3. **Simplify and optimize.** Only after deleting. The common mistake is optimizing something that should not exist.
4. **Accelerate cycle time.** Make the loop faster. Only after simplifying.
5. **Automate.** Last, never first. Automating a bad process makes a fast bad process.

## Traps

- Starting at step 3 or step 5.
- Deleting nothing because everything "might be needed."
