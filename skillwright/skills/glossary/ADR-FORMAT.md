# ADR format

Use this format when a decision meets the skill's three ADR conditions.
Save the ADR in the decision folder declared by the owning workspace.

```markdown
# <number>: <decision title>

## Context

What problem requires a decision? What constraints apply?

## Alternatives

What choices were considered? What does each choice cost or give up?

## Decision

What was decided, by whom, and why?

## Consequences

What improves? What costs, risks, and follow-up work remain?
```
